☀️ Solar tip #029 · For every home

Keep your paperwork

Panel performance warranties typically run 25 to 30 years, inverters 5 to 10. Keep invoices and serial numbers.

Photograph the labels on panels, inverter and battery on installation day and keep them with the invoice. Warranty claims are much easier with proof.
