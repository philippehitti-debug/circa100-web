Charge voltages come from the datasheet

For a 16S LiFePO4 pack, absorption is typically around 56 V, but always use the battery maker's values.

Wrong charge settings are the fastest way to unbalance cells. Set absorption, float and cut-off exactly as the manufacturer specifies.
