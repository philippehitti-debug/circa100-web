☀️ Solar tip #008 · For the technical crowd

Keep DC voltage drop under about 1.5%

Size DC cables for voltage drop, not just ampacity. UV-rated solar cable, 4 or 6 mm² for most residential strings.

Long roof-to-basement runs are common in Lebanese buildings. Calculate the drop for the full loop length (go and return).
