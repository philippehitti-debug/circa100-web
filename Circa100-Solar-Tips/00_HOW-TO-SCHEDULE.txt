CIRCA 100 · SOLAR TIPS — HOW TO SCHEDULE THE 40 POSTS ON INSTAGRAM

Each post has two files with the same number:
  NN_date_day_TIP.jpg  = the image to post
  NN_date_day_TIP.txt  = the caption to copy and paste
00_SCHEDULE.csv lists all 40 posts with dates (Mon/Wed/Fri, 25 Nov 2026 to 24 Feb 2027, 17:45 Beirut).

ONE-TIME CHECK
  @circa.100 must be a Professional account and connected to your Circa 100 Facebook Page
  (Instagram app > Settings > Accounts Center, or Facebook Page settings > Linked accounts).

SCHEDULING (Meta Business Suite, free)
  1. Go to business.facebook.com and choose the Circa 100 business. (On your phone you can use the Meta Business Suite app.)
  2. Left menu > Planner (or Content) > Create post.
  3. Under "Post to", tick Instagram (@circa.100). Untick Facebook unless you also want it on the Page.
  4. Add photo/video > Upload from computer > choose 01_....jpg.
  5. Open 01_....txt, select all (Cmd+A), copy (Cmd+C), and paste it into the text box.
  6. At the bottom, choose "Schedule", set the date and time from the file name (17:45), and click Schedule.
  7. Repeat for 02, 03 ...

  If the Planner won't let you schedule that far ahead, schedule the first month now and the rest later.
  Tip: do 5–10 posts per sitting; each one takes about a minute.
