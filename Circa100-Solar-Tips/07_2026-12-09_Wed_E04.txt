☀️ Solar tip #007 · For every home

One shadow can cost a whole string

A water tank, antenna or satellite dish shading a single panel can pull down the output of every panel wired with it.

Panels in a string behave like a chain: the weakest link sets the pace. Before installing, walk the roof at 9:00, 12:00 and 15:00 and note every shadow. Move small obstacles if you can.
