Space rows for the winter solstice

At Beirut's latitude the noon sun on 21 December is about 33° high. Row gap ≈ 1.55 × the rise of the panel ahead.

Rise = panel length × sin(tilt). Add a margin if you want shade-free hours beyond noon in winter.
