☀️ Solar tip #025 · For every home

After a storm, look but don't touch

Check for cracked glass, loose cables or moved panels. Panel cables are live whenever there is daylight.

Even the switched-off panel side can carry dangerous DC voltage. If something looks wrong, switch off at the inverter and call your installer.
