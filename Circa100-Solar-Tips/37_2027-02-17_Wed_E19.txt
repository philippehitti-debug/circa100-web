☀️ Solar tip #037 · For every home

Put the battery in a cool place

Install batteries in a ventilated, shaded spot away from bedrooms. Heat ages every battery.

LiFePO4 (lithium iron phosphate) is the safer and longer-lasting chemistry for homes. Keep the area clear and dry.
