Battery cables carry the big current

A 5 kW inverter on a 48 V battery draws over 100 A. Size the cable and fuse or breaker at the battery for that.

Undersized battery leads run hot and cause voltage sag and nuisance low-voltage cut-offs. Keep them short, use proper lugs and torque them to spec.
