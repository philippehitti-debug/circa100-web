Design Voc for the coldest morning

Voc rises as cells get colder. Correct string Voc to the site's minimum temperature and stay under the inverter's max DC input.

Use the module's Voc temperature coefficient (typically around −0.25 to −0.3 %/°C). Mountain sites in Lebanon can see sub-zero winter mornings, which pushes Voc well above STC.
