☀️ Solar tip #014 · For the technical crowd

Bond the frames, add surge protection

Earth module frames and rails, and fit Type 2 SPDs on both the DC and AC sides.

Rooftop arrays are exposed to induced surges. Keep earthing conductors short and direct, and check SPD status windows during maintenance.
