☀️ Solar tip #003 · For every home

Run heavy loads between 10:00 and 15:00

Washing machine, water pump, electric heater, ironing: schedule them while the sun is high and your panels are at their peak.

Every kWh you use straight from the panels is a kWh your battery doesn't have to store and your generator doesn't have to make. Timers and smart plugs make this automatic.
