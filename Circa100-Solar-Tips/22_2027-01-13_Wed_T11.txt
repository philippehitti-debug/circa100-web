☀️ Solar tip #022 · For the technical crowd

Use BMS communication, not voltage guesses

Connect lithium batteries over CAN or RS485 and select the matching battery protocol. SOC becomes accurate.

Voltage-based 'user' profiles misread the flat LiFePO4 curve, leading to early cut-offs or overcharging.
