☀️ Solar tip #006 · For the technical crowd

One MPPT, one orientation, one module

Don't mix module models, tilts or azimuths on the same MPPT input. Mismatch costs yield.

Use separate MPPT inputs for east and west arrays, or for strings with different module counts.
