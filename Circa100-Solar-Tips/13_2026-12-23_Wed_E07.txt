☀️ Solar tip #013 · For every home

Expect less in winter, and plan for it

In Lebanon, a December day gives roughly half the energy of a July day: the sun is lower and the days are shorter.

Size your system and your habits for winter, not only for summer. Shift loads to midday, and keep the generator for the gaps.
