☀️ Solar tip #039 · For every home

Hunt the standby drain

A device drawing 20 W around the clock uses about 0.5 kWh a day, straight from your battery at night.

Old receivers, chargers and decoders left on standby add up. Use switched power strips for the TV corner and the office.
