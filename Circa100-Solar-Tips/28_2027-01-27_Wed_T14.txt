☀️ Solar tip #028 · For the technical crowd

Measure every string before connecting

Strings on the same MPPT should show Voc within about 2 to 3% of each other.

A string reading clearly low usually means a missing module, reversed polarity or a bad connector. Fix it before the inverter sees it.
