☀️ Solar tip #027 · For every home

Know where your DC switch is

The DC isolator disconnects the panels from the inverter. Every adult in the house should know where it is.

Ask your installer to label the DC isolator, the AC breaker and the battery breaker clearly. In an emergency, seconds count.
