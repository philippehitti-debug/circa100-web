☀️ Solar tip #019 · For every home

An old fridge can cost you panels

An inefficient fridge can use double the energy of a modern inverter model. Replacing it can be cheaper than adding panels.

Check the energy label. Fridges run day and night, so they matter most for your battery. Keep the door seals clean and the back coil dusted.
