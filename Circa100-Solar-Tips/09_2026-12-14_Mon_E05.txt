☀️ Solar tip #009 · For every home

Check your app, not just your lights

Compare today's kWh with a similar sunny day. A sudden drop usually means dust, new shade or a fault.

Your inverter app keeps daily and monthly history. A quick look once a week catches problems early, before they cost you weeks of lost production.
