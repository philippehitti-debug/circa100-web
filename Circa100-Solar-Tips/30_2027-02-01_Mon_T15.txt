☀️ Solar tip #030 · For the technical crowd

Design for the wind, not the brochure

Ballast or anchor to the calculated wind load for the site. Coastal and mountain roofs see very different loads.

Check the roof slab and parapets. A short structural note for each site protects the client and the contractor.
