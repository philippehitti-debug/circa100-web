☀️ Solar tip #011 · For every home

Hot panels make less power

Solar panels lose roughly 0.3 to 0.4% of output for every degree above 25°C. Spring often beats midsummer.

That is why a bright, cool April day can produce as much as a hot August one. Good airflow behind the panels helps them stay cooler.
