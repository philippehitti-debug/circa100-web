☀️ Solar tip #021 · For every home

Heat water with the midday sun

Put the electric water heater on a timer for late morning and early afternoon, or use a solar thermal heater.

A tank heated at noon stays warm into the evening. A solar thermal collector is still the cheapest way to heat water in Lebanon.
