Wash your panels the smart way

Rinse with plain water early in the morning or at sunset. Never splash cold water on hot glass at midday, and skip soap and hard brushes.

Lebanese dust and pollen settle fast, especially after a dry spell or a sandstorm. A gentle rinse every few weeks keeps output up. A soft mop with a long handle is enough; abrasive pads scratch the anti-reflective coating.
