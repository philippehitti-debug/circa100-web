☀️ Solar tip #020 · For the technical crowd

A DC/AC ratio of 1.1 to 1.3 is normal

Oversizing the array relative to the inverter raises morning and evening yield, with only small midday clipping.

Check the inverter's maximum DC input power and current per MPPT before oversizing.
