☀️ Solar tip #005 · For every home

Don't empty your battery every night

Keep a reserve of about 20% in a lithium battery. Deep daily discharges shorten its life.

Most hybrid inverters let you set a minimum state of charge. Ask your installer to set a sensible cut-off so the battery always has a buffer for the morning and for cloudy days.
