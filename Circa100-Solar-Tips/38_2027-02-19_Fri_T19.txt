☀️ Solar tip #038 · For the technical crowd

Clean with soft water

Hard water leaves scale on the glass that cuts light more than the dust you removed. Use soft or demineralised water.

Avoid high-pressure jets on edges and junction boxes. Clean when the panels are cool.
