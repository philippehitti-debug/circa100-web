☀️ Solar tip #015 · For every home

Size the battery for your nights

List what must run after sunset: fridge, lights, router, TV, a fan. That list sizes your battery, not your whole bill.

Daytime loads should run directly on the panels. A battery sized for the evening and night keeps the investment reasonable and the battery working within its comfort zone.
