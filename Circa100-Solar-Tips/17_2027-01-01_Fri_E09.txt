☀️ Solar tip #017 · For every home

Give your inverter room to breathe

Keep the inverter out of direct sun and out of closed cupboards. Heat makes it reduce its output.

Leave clearance around the unit as the manual specifies, keep the fins dust-free, and never stack boxes against it.
