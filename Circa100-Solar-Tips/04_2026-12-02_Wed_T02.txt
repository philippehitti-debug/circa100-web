Check Vmp at hot cell temperature

At a summer cell temperature of around 70°C, the string Vmp must still sit inside the inverter's MPPT window.

Too few modules per string and the hot-day voltage drops below the tracker's minimum, so the inverter loses the MPP exactly when irradiance is highest.
