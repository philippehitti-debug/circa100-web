☀️ Solar tip #010 · For the technical crowd

Never mix MC4 brands

Mated connectors from different manufacturers are a classic cause of hot joints and fires. Same brand, proper crimp tool.

Cross-mated connectors void warranties and can fail open-circuit or overheat. Crimp, don't solder, and pull-test every joint.
